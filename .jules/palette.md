## 2023-10-27 - [ToolTip hit area expansion]
**Learning:** In WinUI 3 XAML, apply `ToolTipService.ToolTip` to the root container (e.g., `<Grid>`) of a `DataTemplate` to expand the hover target area. However, do NOT apply `AutomationProperties.Name` to non-interactive structural elements like a `Grid` inside a `DataTemplate`, as this is an accessibility anti-pattern that can cause screen readers to incorrectly announce it as interactive or obscure the semantics of its child elements.
**Action:** When implementing micro-UX improvements involving tooltips in list templates, place the tooltip binding on the largest hit-testable container (like a transparent Grid), but carefully avoid adding interactive automation properties to structural elements unless explicitly required.
